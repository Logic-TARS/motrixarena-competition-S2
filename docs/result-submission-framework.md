### Technical Execution & Result Submission Framework: MotrixArena S2 3v3 Humanoid Robot Simulation Challenge

#### 1\. Executive Context: The Embodied AI Soccer Experiment

The MotrixArena S2 is conceived as a premier experimental laboratory for embodied intelligence, utilizing the high-stakes, dynamic environment of 3v3 soccer to push the limits of autonomous decision-making. In this simulation, the soccer pitch is abstracted into a rigorous testing ground where mechanical agility must be seamlessly integrated with cognitive processing. Organized by  **Motphys** , the challenge officially commences on  **June 8, 2026** , with the strategic goal of advancing the state-of-the-art in humanoid robotics through a competitive, high-fidelity framework.The competition prioritizes the synthesis of four high-level technical objectives, addressing the "Core Problems" of modern robotics:

* **Stable Walking:**  Establishing robust locomotive foundations for humanoid agents in a dynamic environment.  
* **Multi-body Coordination:**  Developing sophisticated tactical cooperation and team-based logic between autonomous units.  
* **Real-time Decision-making:**  Executing complex actions and environmental responses in millisecond cycles.  
* **Adversarial Game Theory:**  Navigating competitive dynamics and counter-strategies against high-caliber opponents.This strategic vision requires a disciplined technical operations framework to ensure that every tactical maneuver is a measurable reflection of algorithmic excellence.

#### 2\. Technical Architecture & Simulation Standards

A standardized simulation environment is the cornerstone of the MotrixArena S2, ensuring a "zero hardware barrier" that democratizes participation. By fixing the physical parameters of the challenge, we shift the competitive focus entirely onto algorithmic performance, ensuring that the winners are determined by the superiority of their embodied intelligence systems rather than their access to capital-intensive hardware.The standardized technical stack is defined as follows:| Component | Specification || \------ | \------ || **Simulation Platform** | MotrixSim || **Cloud Training & Evaluation** | MotrixLab \+ RoboGo || **Hardware Model** | Booster K1 Humanoid (Booster Robotics) || **Field Standard** | Replicated RoboCup Standard Field |  
The designation of the  **Booster K1**  as the universal simulation model is a critical architectural choice. By utilizing a digital twin that precisely replicates the K1's mass distribution, joint limits, and torque profiles, we ensure high-fidelity sim-to-real potential. Crucially, the K1 is designed to  **support secondary development** , a feature that informs both the simulation complexity and the practical utility of the physical hardware awarded as prizes. MotrixSim's role in  **replicating**  the RoboCup field ensures that the spatial logic and tactical constraints remain consistent with international robotic sports standards.

#### 3\. Operational Lifecycle: Milestone Schedule

The competition follows a phased operational approach designed to filter for technical robustness while maximizing professional engagement. This structure ensures a rigorous evaluation of every team’s submission.The chronological timeline for the 2026 season is as follows:

* **The Preliminary Phase**  
* **Registration (5/1 – 5/15):**  Public registration period, requiring the recording of team information and a formal  **registration of participation intent**  to facilitate early screening.  
* **Work Submission (5/15 – 6/5):**  The primary technical gate. Teams must submit finalized RL action models and technical documentation.  
* **The Competitive Phase**  
* **Qualifying Scores (6/8 – 6/12):**  Initial  **automated validation (海选跑分)**  on the RoboGo platform to determine the top 32 teams based on objective performance metrics.  
* **Group Stages (6/12 – 6/24):**  8 groups compete in a single round-robin format (48 matches total), with four matches broadcast daily.  
* **Announcement of Finalists (6/25):**  Publication of the Top 16 and the official elimination bracket.  
* **The Elite Phase**  
* **16 to 8 Elimination (6/27 – 6/28):**  Weekend broadcasts featuring eight head-to-head matches.  
* **8 to 4 Quarterfinals (6/30):**  High-intensity execution of four matches in a single day.  
* **Semifinals (7/3):**  The final four teams compete for championship berths.  
* **Grand Final (7/5):**  The championship match, featuring an  **All-Star exhibition with an Audience Public Voting Mechanism** , followed by the awards ceremony.This schedule serves as a rigorous filter, ensuring that only the most resilient and adaptable algorithms advance to the live broadcast stages.

#### 4\. Standardized Result Submission Specifications

The submission process acts as the critical interface between the developer's local environment and the cloud-based evaluation engine. To ensure technical compliance, every submission must include two mandatory components:

1. **Reinforcement Learning (RL) Action Models:**  The behavioral core of the agent, dictating all locomotive and tactical actions.  
2. **Technical Solution Documentation:**  A detailed blueprint of the architectural approach, training parameters, and logic used.The  **RoboGo Cloud Platform**  serves as the automated validation engine, ingesting these submissions to generate high-fidelity performance scores. This requirement forces a balance between coding excellence and documentation clarity, ensuring that winning solutions are both performant and intellectually transparent. These submissions are the data points that RoboGo transforms into the measurable metrics used to rank teams during the qualifying rounds.

#### 5\. Competitive Mechanics & Evaluation Rubric

Matches are structured as two 10-minute halves within a 3v3 format, designed to stress-test both individual agent control and collective tactical synchronization. In the event of a tie during elimination rounds, teams enter a  **10-minute high-frequency penalty shootout** . This high-frequency format is specifically intended to stress-test the rapid reset capabilities and response cycles of the RL agents under extreme pressure.Evaluation is conducted across three fundamental dimensions:| Dimension | Definition | Strategic Impact || \------ | \------ | \------ || **Autonomous Control** | Maneuvering (walking, running, kicking) without manual intervention. | Determines the fundamental mobility and reliability of the individual agent. || **Multi-robot Coordination** | Tactical cooperation, passing, and formation synchronization. | Acts as the primary differentiator in elite play; determines team efficiency. || **Model Robustness** | Algorithmic stability across varying adversarial scenarios. | Ensures functional stability against diverse and unpredictable opponent strategies. |  
This multi-dimensional rubric ensures that the ultimate champion demonstrates a comprehensive mastery of embodied intelligence.

#### 6\. Qualification Standards & Team Composition

The MotrixArena S2 maintains high technical benchmarks by curating a diverse and elite participant pool.

* **Target Audience:**  Open to university students (undergraduate and graduate), embodied AI researchers, and the open-source development community.  
* **The Dual-Track System:**  
* **Invited Track:**  Reserved for 8 "Seed" teams with proven RoboCup experience, setting a professional baseline for the competition.  
* **Open Track:**  Public channel for independent developers to qualify through the automated scoring phase.  
* **Team Constraints:**  Each team is limited to a maximum of 4 members and 2 mentors to ensure concentrated development focus.The presence of  **Seed Teams**  ensures a "hardcore" benchmark, challenging all participants to elevate their reinforcement learning models to professional competitive standards.
